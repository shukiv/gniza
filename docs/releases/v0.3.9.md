# v0.3.9

## The pages are Gniza's own design

v0.3.8 put the pages on daisyUI in daisyUI's stock colours. This release
gives them their own theme and the layout of the approved redesign.

The palette is cool greys with a blue bias, a marine-ink primary and teal
as the accent; success, warning and error stay their own colours, so a red
row is a failure and never a button. Dark follows the same rules on a
near-black ground. The rail's theme switch, and the "system" choice, work
as before. Type is a size smaller and tables a row shorter: an operator
with three hundred accounts sees more of them.

The overview leads with the verdict sentence, the last and next run under
it, and the buttons that answer it. A strip of four cards follows: accounts
with a good recent backup, data under management, accounts restore-tested,
and what the destinations hold. Below, two columns: what is worth acting
on and what has happened on the left; where backups go, with a fill bar
per destination, the schedules and staging space on the right.

Destinations are cards rather than rows, every action on the card. The
accounts table filters with a joined strip of chips and acts with a joined
strip of buttons. A running job's row is blue; a failed one red; a stale
one amber, as a stripe down the row's leading edge. The customer's pages
lead with whether the account is protected and three facts under it.

Nothing about what a page does has changed: every form, link and dialog
posts what it posted.

## Under it

The themes are two `@plugin "daisyui/theme"` blocks in
`internal/webui/ui/input.css` (ADR 0023, amended). The host isolation and
the `cpr:` namespace are unchanged, and were measured again inside WHM's
chrome: no property on any element of ours is set by WHM.
