This removes the history ticked below from this server. It cannot be undone. The backups themselves are not touched: every snapshot stays at its destination and can be restored.

A few rows stay, because they are more than a log: the last run and the last good copy of each account at each destination, which is what the overview and termination protection read to say an account is protected; the last rehearsal; a download still waiting to be collected; and anything still running. A search does not narrow this: what is ticked is cleared whatever is showing. The service log is the system journal's and is not cleared from here.
